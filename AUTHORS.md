# Authorship and AI assistance

The author and initiator of Pislia Dzvonyka (“Після дзвоника”, After the Bell) is **Viktor Vyhivskyi**, software developer: https://vyhivskyi.com/.

Viktor defined the idea, requirements and user journeys, made product decisions, configured local development and hosting, and reviewed the site's behavior.
